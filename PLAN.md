## Now

### SRV-03 — Campaigns, sessions and scenes over REST

- **Outcome:** with a DM session, the DM can create, rename, list, reorder and delete campaigns, sessions and scenes, and duplicate a scene with its tokens. Deletions cascade after a confirmation that states what will be removed, and deleting the live scene or an ancestor clears the live scene.
- **Specs:** `13` §4 SRV-03, `02` §5 (`/api/campaigns`, `/api/campaigns/:id/sessions`, `/api/sessions/:id/scenes`), `03` §2 (order within the parent), `03` §3 (server-generated UUIDs), `03` §5 (grid preset copied on creation), `03` §6 (a scene without a map, 30 × 20), `03` §7 (cascades, confirmation, live-scene deletion, duplication); D-050, D-075.
- **Dependencies:** SRV-01 (schema), SRV-02 (session guard). Images arrive with SRV-04, so tests insert image rows directly to create scenes with a map.
- **Acceptance (executable):**
  - Vitest against a real SQLite file: create, read, rename and list each entity, with ids generated by the server as lowercase UUIDs; a body naming its own id, or with an unknown field, is refused and stores nothing.
  - Sessions within a campaign and scenes within a session keep a unique order. A reorder applies in two steps under the unique constraint (D-075) and leaves the order gap-free; a reorder naming a foreign or missing id is refused and changes nothing.
  - A new scene with a map copies that image's grid preset; a scene without a map stores 30 × 20.
  - Duplicating a scene creates a new scene with its own copies of every token, under new ids.
  - Deleting a session removes its scenes and tokens; deleting a campaign removes its sessions, scenes and tokens, only after a confirmation step that states how many of each will go. Deleting the live scene or an ancestor warns that it is live and clears `live_scene_id`.
  - Every route answers 401 without a DM session (the identical-answer test of SRV-02 picks the routes up from `app.declaredRoutes`); every read and write names its columns.
  - `make verify` exit 0 on both CI runners; `TRACEABILITY.md` SRV-03 row with test names.
- **Non-goals:** scene setup and tokens while not live (`/api/scenes/:id`, PRP-02 to PRP-04), image upload (SRV-04), the sidebar UI (PRP-01), live-scene activation (LIV-04).
- **Review:** `Surfaces: data`, `Touches red line: yes`, `Contract change: yes`, so prompt 2 (review) runs after implementation.
